Before your reading

A little preparation.
A few answers.

From birth details to booking updates, here’s what helps before your consultation.

Browse all questions below.

What birth details do I need?

Your date of birth, local birth time, and the city where you were born. If the time is uncertain, speak to us before paying so we can explain the limitations.

Can I consult remotely?

Contact us to agree on the format and arrange a suitable time. You can also visit the Jalandhar office with a confirmed appointment.

How is my payment confirmed?

Razorpay handles checkout. Our server verifies the payment and booking details before showing a confirmed status. If the status is pending, check again or contact us with the booking reference. Do not pay again just because a window closed.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please contact us as early as possible. Requests made at least 24 hours before the session are eligible for rescheduling or a full service credit. Late cancellations and no-shows are non-refundable. Any approved refund is handled through the original payment method; see our booking terms.

Does the free chart use AI?

No. It uses a deterministic astronomy library and chart calculations on your device. It does not send your birth details to an AI service. The results show the calculation settings and limits.

Will a reading guarantee a particular outcome?

No. Astrology is a traditional and spiritual practice. We do not guarantee outcomes, and it does not replace qualified medical, financial, legal, or mental-health advice.

When will my Teva arrive?

The digital Teva is normally prepared within 24 hours after the required details are received. For a physical Teva, we confirm preparation and delivery estimates with you. Contact us before ordering if you need it by a particular date.

What if I don’t know my exact birth time?

Please speak to us before booking or choosing an approximate time in the calculator. A small change in birth time can affect the ascendant and chart interpretation. We’ll explain what can and cannot be considered with the information you have.

Can I prepare someone else’s chart?

Only share another person’s birth details with their permission. For marriage matching, contact us if you need help arranging how to share the second person’s details.

Where is the office, and when can I visit?

Our office is at 124 Janta Colony, Jalandhar, Punjab 144008. Visiting hours are Monday–Wednesday and Friday–Saturday, 10 am–1 pm and 3–5:30 pm; Thursday, 3–5 pm. We are closed on Sunday. Please confirm an appointment before travelling.
